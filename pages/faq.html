More Questions Answered

How do I sell my house fast in London? The fastest route is a direct cash buyer rather than an open-market listing. Instead of marketing your property for weeks and waiting on a buyer's mortgage and chain, we buy directly with our own funds — survey within 24 hours, formal written offer the same day, and exchange as fast as 24 hours after that. To sell your house fast in London, request a free valuation and we'll confirm exactly how quickly we can move on your specific property.

Will you buy my property if it needs renovation? Yes. We buy properties in any condition — including those requiring full refurbishment, with structural issues, damp, or short leases. We do not reduce our offer based on condition unless something material is discovered during the survey that was not disclosed.

Can you buy my property if it is tenanted? Yes. We purchase tenanted properties, including AST, regulated, and periodic tenancies. You do not need to serve notice before we complete. We handle all tenant-related matters after completion.

What is the difference between your offer and open market value? We typically offer 75-85% of open market value. In exchange, you receive certainty of sale, no fees (saving approximately 2-3% in estate agent fees and 1-2% in legal costs), and a guaranteed timeline. For many sellers, the net proceeds compare favourably to a conventional sale when delays and costs are factored in.

Is this the same as a cash buyer I might find on Rightmove? No. We are a direct buyer using our own funds — not a broker who connects you with third-party investors. This removes an additional layer of uncertainty and means we can exchange much faster.
