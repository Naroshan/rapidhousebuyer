Who We Are

Rapid House Buyer is a privately funded direct cash buyer, not a broker or middleman. We purchase residential properties directly using our own capital — no mortgage approvals, no funding conditions, no third-party investors. This is why we can exchange in as little as 24 hours.

Our Credentials

We are full members of the National Association of Property Buyers (NAPB), registered with The Property Ombudsman (TPO), and ICO registered. Our in-house RICS-accredited surveyors enable same-day valuations across England and Wales.

Our Promise

We make one offer per property. If you accept, we honour it. There are no last-minute reductions, no phantom buyers, and no chains. Since 2012 we have purchased over 347 properties across England and Wales with a 4.9-star average rating from verified sellers. The price we agree on day one is the price you receive on completion.
