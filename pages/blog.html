London Property Market Insights

Understanding the London property market helps sellers make informed decisions. Whether you are facing repossession, managing an inherited estate, or simply want to release equity quickly, staying informed about market conditions helps you evaluate your options clearly.

Why Speed Matters When Selling

In most distressed sale situations, time is the primary constraint. Every month of delay on a property with mortgage arrears adds costs and risk. A cash buyer removes the uncertainty of a conventional sale — no surveys to renegotiate, no chains to collapse, no buyer to change their mind.

What to Expect from a Cash Sale

A legitimate cash buyer will make a formal written offer within 24-48 hours of a free survey, honour that offer without late-stage reductions, and complete within a timescale agreed at the outset. Any buyer who reduces the price at the last moment or charges fees is not operating to industry standards.
