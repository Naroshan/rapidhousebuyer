How It Works

Our process is designed to be fast, transparent, and stress-free. From first contact to cash in your account in as little as 7 days.

Step by Step

Step 1 — Contact us: Call 020 7199 1698, WhatsApp, or complete the form on our homepage. A senior consultant will call back within 2 hours to discuss your property and situation. There is no obligation at this stage and no information you provide will be shared with third parties.

Step 2 — Free RICS survey: We arrange a survey at a time that suits you — often the same day for London properties. Our in-house surveyors assess the property thoroughly and provide us with an independent valuation. The survey is completely free and does not commit you to anything.

Step 3 — Formal cash offer: Within 24 hours of the survey, we issue a formal written cash offer. This is our real offer — not an inflated figure that will be reduced later. We make one offer per property and we honour it.

Step 4 — Exchange and completion: Our solicitors handle all the legal work. You instruct your own solicitor (we cover your legal costs). We exchange contracts on a date you agree to, then complete on a date that suits you — as fast as the next working day after exchange, or longer if you need more time. Funds are transferred directly to your account or solicitor on the day of completion.

To get started, request your free cash offer or call us on 020 7199 1698.

How to Value My House in the UK

Wondering what your house is worth? Our free house valuation is carried out by in-house RICS-accredited surveyors, not an algorithm or a desktop estimate — the same standard of property valuation used across London and the rest of the UK for mortgage lending. There is no cost and no obligation to sell once you have your figure.

Because we buy directly with our own funds, we can usually arrange your house valuation within 24 hours of first contact, often the same day in London. You will receive a formal written cash offer based on that valuation, not a vague estimate that changes later.

Get your free house valuation or call us on 020 7199 1698.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the whole process take from start to finish? As fast as 7 days from first contact to funds in your account, though the exact timeline is set by you — exchange can happen the next working day after your survey if you need to move quickly, or later if you need more time.

Do I need to pay for the survey or valuation? No. The RICS survey and valuation are completely free and carry no obligation to proceed — you only commit once you accept a formal written offer.
