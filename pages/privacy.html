Data We Collect

We collect name, phone, email, and property postcode when you submit an enquiry. This information is used solely to provide you with a cash offer and to communicate about your property sale.

We never sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting enquiries@rapidhousebuyer.co.uk. We are registered with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office).

To get started, request your free cash offer or call us on 020 7199 1698.

What Data We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property postcode, and reason for selling. This information is used solely to provide you with a cash offer and to communicate about your property sale. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to: contact you with a cash offer; arrange a survey if you wish to proceed; complete the property transaction; and comply with legal and regulatory requirements (including HMRC reporting for property transactions). We retain your data for 7 years in accordance with financial regulation requirements.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations); object to processing; and request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at enquiries@rapidhousebuyer.co.uk. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
