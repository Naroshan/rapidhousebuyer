How a Fast Sale Stops Repossession

When a lender issues a repossession notice or applies for a possession order, a fast cash sale can intercept the process at any stage — even with a court date already set — by clearing the outstanding mortgage debt before the hearing takes place.

What Happens If You Do Nothing

If repossession proceeds, the lender sells the property at auction for significantly below market value, then pursues you for any shortfall plus their legal costs. The repossession remains on your credit file for six years, affecting your ability to borrow or rent in future.

How We Help

Frequently Asked Questions

How late can I act before repossession is finalised? There is no fixed cut-off — even with a possession order granted or a court date already set, a fast cash sale can often still intercept the process by clearing the mortgage debt before the lender takes the property. The earlier you contact us, the more options remain, but do not assume it is too late.

Will I still owe money after repossession? Potentially, yes. If the lender sells at auction for less than the outstanding mortgage, you remain liable for the shortfall plus their legal costs, and the repossession stays on your credit file for six years. A cash sale before that point avoids the shortfall entirely and you keep whatever equity remains.
