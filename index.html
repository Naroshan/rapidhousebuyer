Straight Talk

Here's Exactly How We're Honest With You

This industry has a poor reputation. Being straightforward with you — even when it costs us a sale — is how we intend to earn your trust.

We typically offer 75–85% of open market value

That's the trade-off for speed, certainty and zero costs. We won't dress this up as "market value" — it isn't, and we'll always say so plainly, upfront.

If the open market suits you better, we'll say so

If you're not under time pressure and your property would likely sell well through an estate agent, we'll tell you — even though it means you don't use us.

No pressure tactics, ever

No fake countdown timers, no "this offer expires tonight." Take whatever time you need, and please speak to an independent solicitor or financial adviser before deciding.

We are not FCA regulated

Buying residential property for cash is not an FCA-regulated activity in the UK. We handle your data in line with UK GDPR and encourage independent legal advice.
