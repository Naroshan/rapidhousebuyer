Common questions

Questions, Answered.

How much below market value will you offer?

Cash buyers typically offer between 75–85% of open market value in exchange for guaranteed speed, certainty, and removal of all costs. We are transparent about our valuations and encourage independent advice. If the open market would genuinely suit you better, we will tell you so.

Can you really exchange in 24 hours?

Yes, in genuine urgency cases — particularly where a repossession hearing is imminent. This depends on title documents being available. For most cases, exchange occurs within 3–7 working days, with completion on any date you choose.

Do you buy properties with sitting tenants?

Yes. We regularly purchase tenanted properties — including ASTs, periodic tenancies, and regulated tenancies — and manage tenant communications ourselves. We also purchase HMOs, multi-unit properties, and full portfolios.

Is there any obligation after getting a cash offer?

None. Our initial offer is free and without obligation. We encourage independent legal and financial advice, and we do not use pressure tactics or impose expiry deadlines.

Are you regulated by the FCA?

No. Buying residential property for cash is not an FCA-regulated activity. We handle personal data in line with UK GDPR.
